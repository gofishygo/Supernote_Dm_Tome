# Session: The Larders of Ill Omen

Below stairs (K61 to K72). Kitchens, cellars, guard rooms, and the people
who have kept this house running for a master who does not eat.

Manners are the engine. Down here the party is either guests or intruders,
and the servants decide which. Guests are fed and shown about. Intruders
are reported.

Start them as guests. Each rude, violent or thieving act in front of a
servant ticks one of three boxes. At three they are intruders, and the
dead soldiers in the guard rooms get up.

Room numbers are from the book. It has the last word on traps and stats.

## Strong start: The lift that is not a lift

> A plain stone corridor, and under your feet a soft click. The walls
> begin to move. No. The floor does. A sweet smell comes up through the
> cracks, and the ceiling is getting closer very quickly.

K61, the elevator trap. Run the gas and the ride from the book. Used well
it splits the party. Used kindly it only frightens them.

- Abraham hears the click a breath early. He may shout one word before it goes.
- Whoever is caught rides to the top of the castle asleep. Whoever is not hears them go.
- Gertruda does not breathe. "Observation: you have all fallen over. Query: is this the plan?"
- If the party just came from the spires, run it backward. The trap is how they arrive down here, waking in a heap with a cook standing over them.

## Scene: The kitchen (K62, K65)

> Heat, steam, and the first good smell in this castle. A huge pot is on
> the boil. The cook turns round, and he is put together wrong: one arm
> furred, one ear like a bat's, a smile with too many kinds of teeth.
> He is overjoyed to see you.

Cyrus Belview, the Count's servant. Cheerful, loyal, quite mad, and the
most useful person in the building if treated as staff treat a guest.

- He offers food. Do not say what is in the pot. Let them ask.
- He will show guests to a room, the wine, the way down, anywhere they name. He will not go into the catacombs.
- He reports everything to the master, without malice, the way a clock reports the time.
- He has served long enough to remember Gertruda arriving. "The young miss! You've had work done."
- Torvald understands him at once. Every hall has one man who really runs it. Persuasion at advantage if Torvald speaks to him as a chief speaks to a steward.
- Striking Cyrus ticks two boxes. Everything down here is fond of him.

## Scene: The wine cellar (K63)

> Barrels the size of carts, racked in the dark. The floor is sticky.
> Somewhere a tap is dripping, slow as a clock.

The casks carry the Wizard of Wines mark. One of them is not holding wine.
Run the cellar's surprise from the book.

- If the party helped the Martikovs, they know these labels, and know one vintage has not been delivered in years.
- Batrdlebee, Perception DC 12: one barrel sounds wrong when knocked. Full, but not of liquid.
- Drinking the Count's wine as his guest is fine. Carrying it off ticks a box.
- Hidden behind the last rack, a hunter's cache: stakes, a mirror, holy water gone flat. Esmeralda's, from an earlier visit, or someone's who did not come back for it.

## Scene: The hall of bones (K67)

> A dining hall for soldiers, and every stick of furniture in it is
> bone. Chairs of ribs, a table of thigh bones lashed with wire, a
> chandelier of skulls. Over the far door hangs the skull of a dragon,
> long as a boat.

The dragon is Argynvost. His skull belongs in the beacon of his own keep,
and the knights there cannot rest without it.

- If the party has been to Argynvostholt, they know exactly what this is. If not, Abraham knows no hunter ever took a trophy like that.
- It weighs 250 pounds. Getting it out of the castle is a session of its own.
- Taking it down ticks every box at once. The household may forgive theft. It does not forgive this one.
- Torvald looks at a hall furnished with the enemy dead and understands the man who built it better than he wants to.
- Evander can feel that none of these bones are at rest. Turn Undead in here makes the chairs flinch.

## Scene: The guard rooms (K68 to K71)

> Bunks in rows, blankets squared, boots under each bed. On every bunk
> lies a soldier, hands folded, long past dead. Their weapons are oiled.

The Count's old garrison. They lie still for guests. They rise for
intruders, or if anyone takes a weapon from the racks.

- Skeletons: AC 13, HP 13. Use eight. In the officers' hall add two wights, AC 14, HP 45.
- They fight as a unit: shield line at the door, archers behind. Torvald will recognise drilled men.
- Evander's Turn Undead breaks the line. This is his room.
- If the party are still guests, an officer sits up, salutes Torvald as a visiting lord, and lies back down.
- Their banner is the raven of the old kingdom, from before the Count was anything but a general.

## Scene: The man who keeps the books (K72)

> A cramped office heaped with ledgers to the ceiling. At a desk sits a
> thin old man with a quill, chained by one ankle to the floor. He does
> not look up. "If it's about the candle allowance, I've told Cyrus."

Lief Lipsiege, accountant. Chained here for so long he has stopped minding.
He knows where everything in the castle is kept, because he wrote it down.

- He wants company and a small kindness. He is not sure he wants freedom.
- For either, he will look up one thing in his books. The treasury, the heart, the crypt of any name they give him.
- Abraham asks about the ledger of hunters. Lief keeps the fair copy. He turns to the family's page and goes quiet. There is an entry for an ancestor, marked received, and a crypt number.
- He has an entry for Gertruda too. It is filed under repairs.
- He has an entry for the Tome. It is filed under missing, in fresh ink, and he looks at the party over his spectacles.
- Freeing him ticks a box. He will thank them and then sit back down.

## Scene: The household turns

Run this when the third box is ticked.

> The kitchen fire goes out. All through the cellars, in the dark, comes
> the sound of boots being put on.

- The garrison rises and holds the stairs up. The only open way is down.
- Cyrus is heartbroken. "And I'd made up the good room."
- Rahadin comes from above with the roll of the guard. He is not here to talk this time.
- The chase ends at the dungeon stair, with the party going where they were always going to go.

## Secrets and clues

- Cyrus has seen the Count carry a coffin down to the catacombs by himself, twice a century, and come back up alone.
- The garrison died in one night, at their posts, on the wedding day.
- Lief's books show the castle buys nothing and sells nothing. Only the wine comes in.
- A hunter was received here generations ago and lies in the catacombs under a name Abraham knows.
- Gertruda's making was billed to the household. Lief knows who was paid, and it was not the Count.
- The dragon's skull was hung by Strahd himself, the day he took the valley. It is the only thing in the castle he ever nailed up with his own hands.
- The gas trap was built to catch thieves alive. He likes to talk to them first.

## Treasure and rewards

- A cask of the good vintage, if they are still guests when they leave. Cyrus insists.
- The hunter's cache: four stakes, a silvered mirror, two flasks of holy water that Evander can bless again.
- One true answer from Lief's ledgers.
- The skull of Argynvost, if they can carry it, and the gratitude of an order of dead knights.
