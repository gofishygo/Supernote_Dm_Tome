# Session N: Title of the session

One or two lines of intro: where the party is, what the night is about, the
tone you want. This becomes the first page when you insert the whole session.

## Strong start: One line that starts in motion

> Read-aloud text goes in a blockquote. It is inserted in italics so you can
> find it at a glance. Keep it to three or four sentences.

What happens if the players do nothing. What happens if they act. The one
thing you need to remember about this opening.

## Scene: Name the place or the beat

> Read-aloud for arriving here, if any.

The purpose of the scene in one sentence.

- Bullet facts the players can discover.
- A secret or clue that points onward.
- The NPC in the room and what they want.

Possible outcomes, one line each.

## Scene: Second scene

Scenes do not have to be in order. Write the ones you think you need and
skip the ones you can improvise.

## Scene: Spare scene for if things go sideways

A fight, a chase, a complication you can drop in anywhere.

## Secrets and clues

Ten things the players might learn tonight. Only the ones they find matter.

- Clue one.
- Clue two.

## Treasure and rewards

- What is in the chest.
- What the quest pays.
