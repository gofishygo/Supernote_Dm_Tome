# Torvald

Chief of a people, and the party's front line. He reads a hall the way a
hunter reads a trail: who really runs it, who is watching, when the lord is
busy.

## Hooks in Ravenloft

- Strahd addresses him as one ruler to another: "You lead a people. So do I."
- The stone king in the hall of heroes speaks to him unasked: "I led ten thousand. Count who stands with me now."
- King Barov's tomb is the answer to that line: a king who built a kingdom and a son who buried it.
- The garrison officer salutes him as a visiting lord. Drilled men recognise him.
- Cyrus the cook responds to him as a steward to a chief: Persuasion at advantage when he speaks that way.

## Wants

- To bring his people through, which tonight means bringing this party through.
- To be seen as a leader by the only other ruler in the valley, and to be ashamed of wanting that.

## Secrets the DM holds

- The hall of bones is a hall furnished with the enemy dead. He understands the man who built it better than he wants to.
- The Count's goal for him: see if Torvald bends.

## Table notes

- Spotlight: the stone king, the garrison, the king's tomb, the broom.
- He will want to jump for the Heart of Sorrow. Tell him the odds, then let him.
- The bridge in the rain is his kind of fight: footing, no rail, a soldier on foot at the far end.
