# Abraham

A hunter from a family of hunters, four years on the road to reach this
door. His name is in the Count's ledger, and it is not struck through.

## At a glance

- Vampire hunter by family trade; Investigation and Survival; a suit built for this work.
- Reads hunters' chalk marks (Esmeralda's) and knows a baited snare on sight.
- Class: (check) ranger, fighter or a hunter build; the scripts give him advantage against baited traps and the training to read a vampire's patience.
- Player: (fill in)

## Hooks in Ravenloft

- The study ledger of hunter families: most struck through. His family is there, not struck, with a date beside it. The date is this year.
- Strahd: "Four years on the road, is it not? I have guests who have stayed four hundred."
- Esmeralda's chalk mark on the study door frame: safe for now, do not linger.
- The dead adventurer in the false treasury wears a wolf's-tooth hunter token. So does Esmeralda. So does his family.
- A statue in the hall of heroes has his jaw and brow. A hunter of his blood came here and stayed.
- Lief's fair copy of the ledger: an ancestor marked received, with a crypt number.
- A crypt with his family name: bones laid out with respect, a silvered weapon, a letter that begins, "If you are reading this, he let me write it."

## Wants

- To finish what his family started, and to learn what happened to the one who got this far.
- To decide what a hunter does with Emil, a werewolf unarmed behind bars and asking for help.

## Secrets the DM holds

- Strahd knew a hunter was coming. The ledger was open before dinner.
- The Count respects very few people. The buried ancestor was one.
- Strahd's goal for him: learn the family's secrets.

## Table notes

- He hears the elevator trap a breath early: one word before it goes.
- Give him advantage to spot the treasury snare, and a check to see the catacomb floor is too clean.
- Spotlight: the ledger, the chalk mark, the statue, the crypt, the coffin ("stake, sunlight, running water").
