# Evander

Half vampire, wholly devout. His pulse falls into step with the organ and he
hates that it does. The castle keeps asking him who made him.

## At a glance

- Dhampir; holy symbol; Turn Undead; Medicine and Arcana (from the scripts).
- Class: (check) cleric or paladin; the scripts use Turn Undead and a blessing of holy water.
- Player: (fill in)

## Hooks in Ravenloft

- Strahd, quietly: "Who made you? I should like to know which of my kind was so careless."
- Escher: "Half! How economical. Do you get half the hunger, or all of it and half the teeth?"
- The bath: he smells it from the corridor. Ask his player what the hunger feels like, and whether the others notice him swallow.
- The witches have been asked to brew something for a guest with half a vampire's blood.
- The Heart of Sorrow beats with his pulse: every hit on it costs him a hit point, and he has advantage against it.
- The statues in the hall of heroes turn their eyes away from him, every one.

## Wants

- An answer to who made him, and the strength not to need it.
- To be useful against the dead without becoming more like them.

## Secrets the DM holds

- Sergei's tomb is the one place in the castle at peace. Evander feels it. A long rest there is safe.
- His symbol is cold on the catacombs' wrong consecrated ground. It still works.
- The Count's goal for him: find out who sired him.

## Table notes

- Turn Undead is his room in the guard barracks and buys two rounds in the drowned chamber. On Strahd in his own house it earns a smile.
- Spoiling the coffin's earth with holy water is his rite: three uninterrupted rounds.
- Spotlight: the bath, the Count's question, the heart, the tomb.
