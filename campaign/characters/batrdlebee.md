# Batrdlebee

Halfling bard. Hears the shape of a piece of music and wants, very badly, to
ring the bell.

## At a glance

- Halfling (bell rope "at halfling height"; swimming in three feet of water).
- Bard: Performance and History; passes as a witches' customer; reads verse aloud and gets the rhyme.
- Spelling: the scripts say Batrdlebee; the player may spell it Bardlebee. (check)
- Player: (fill in)

## Hooks in Ravenloft

- The organ: Performance or History DC 13 tells him it is a long requiem, an hour left, and not for the Count himself.
- Strahd: "You heard where I stopped. Tell me how it should end." A good answer buys the party a favour.
- Escher trades truth for stories, and Batrdlebee gets two answers per story.
- The brazier verse: he reads it aloud and gets the rhyme, advantage on choosing right.
- The bell in the belfry, tied in a neat bow, within his reach. If it rings, every box ticks.

## Wants

- To finish the Count's unfinished music, or to be the one who gets to say how it ends.
- An audience, including the one at the organ.

## Secrets the DM holds

- The bell has not rung since the wedding day.
- Strahd's goal for him: win the bard.

## Table notes

- Spotlight: the bell, the unfinished music, Escher's gossip, the verse.
- Let him choose about the bell. Do not take it away from him.
- One barrel in the wine cellar sounds wrong when knocked: Perception DC 12 is his.
