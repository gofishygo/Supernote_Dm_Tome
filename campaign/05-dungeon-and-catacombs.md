# Session: The Dungeon and the Catacombs

The bottom of Castle Ravenloft (K73 to K88). Flooded cells, then the long
dark where the family is buried and the Count sleeps.

The dead are the engine. Every crypt is a door with a name on it, and every
door opened lets something out: a fact, a treasure, or a tenant. The party
chooses how many.

Count the crypts they open. The first three are free. From the fourth on,
each one also wakes something elsewhere in the catacombs, and it comes
looking.

Room numbers are from the book. It has the last word on traps and stats.

## Strong start: The water is waist deep

> The stair ends in black water, cold enough to hurt. An arched hall
> runs off into the dark with barred doors down both sides. Something
> under the surface shifts against your leg, and from one of the cells a
> man's voice says, very quietly, "Don't walk in the middle."

K73. The flooded hall hides trapdoors that drop walkers into the cells. Run
them from the book.

- The water is three feet deep. Batrdlebee is swimming. Someone should offer a shoulder, and Torvald has two.
- The voice is Emil, in the north cells. He is telling the truth about the floor.
- Gertruda: "Complaint: I rust. Statement: one of you will carry me. Clarification: this is not a request."
- Whoever falls lands in a locked cell alone. Getting them out is the first puzzle of the night.

## Scene: The prisoner (K74)

> A big man, half starved, gripping the bars. His eyes catch your light
> and throw it back green, the way a dog's do.

Emil Toranescu. A werewolf, locked up by Strahd for defying the pack's
leader. He wants out, and he wants his wife.

- He admits what he is if asked directly. He will not hide it from a hunter, because a hunter would know.
- Abraham was raised to kill exactly this. Emil is unarmed, behind bars, and asking for help. Let the table sit in that.
- If they met the werewolves or Zuleika, Emil is the husband she is waiting for.
- Freed, he fights beside them until they are out of the castle. Werewolf: AC 12, HP 58. Then he goes home, whatever home has become.
- Left behind, he does not beg. He says, "Then tell her where I am."
- Gertruda has an opinion. "Recommendation: release the wolf. Rationale: it will bite someone, and it might be someone I dislike."

## Scene: The drowned chamber (K76, K77)

> A wide room, flooded like the rest. Racks and iron frames stand up out
> of the water. On a balcony above sit two tall chairs, placed for a
> good view.

The torture chamber. The dead under the water rise when someone reaches
the middle of the room.

- Strahd zombies: AC 8, HP 30. Use six. Run their coming-apart from the book. The parts keep fighting.
- The water is difficult terrain for everyone on foot and for none of them.
- Evander's Turn Undead buys two clear rounds. Gunfire does little. Fire does nothing down here.
- The balcony is dry and climbable, Athletics DC 12. From there the room is a shooting gallery.
- One chair is worn. The other has never been sat in.

## Scene: The brazier (K78)

> A square room and a brazier burning with white fire that gives no
> heat. Around its rim sit seven stones, each a different colour. An
> hourglass hangs above it, and the sand is running.

The castle's shortcut. A stone thrown in the flame sends the thrower
somewhere. Run the colours and the guardians from the book.

- Abraham, Arcana or Investigation DC 14: there is a verse cut round the rim. Each colour names a place.
- This is the fast way to the heart, the study, the chapel, or out. It is also how the party gets separated if they throw at random.
- Batrdlebee reads the verse aloud and gets the rhyme. Advantage on choosing right.
- The hourglass is for the visitor, not the master. When it runs out, the guardians wake.

## Scene: The catacombs (K84)

> The ceiling is alive. Bats, thousands, packed like fruit. The floor
> is soft with what they leave. Away into the dark run rows of stone
> doors, each with a name cut over it, and you cannot see where they
> end.

Forty crypts. Do not run them all. Offer the names, let the party choose,
and count what they open.

- Loud noise brings the bats down. Everyone is blinded and deafened for a round. Gertruda's pistols are loud.
- Near the stair is a snare that swaps a walker with a wight sealed in a crypt. Run it from the book. Abraham gets a check to see the floor is too clean.
- Wight: AC 14, HP 45. Use these as what wakes from the fourth crypt on.
- Evander is on consecrated ground of the wrong kind. His holy symbol is cold. It still works.

Crypts worth offering by name. Check each number in the book.

- A hunter with Abraham's family name. Inside, bones laid out with respect, a silvered weapon of your choice, and a letter that begins, "If you are reading this, he let me write it."
- Patrina Velikovna. A banshee. Her wail can drop a character outright, Con save DC 13. Warn them with cold and weeping first. She wants to be reunited with her brother Kasimir.
- A general called Mad Dog. He kept hounds. They are still here. Hell hounds: AC 15, HP 45.
- A bride, walled in for boring him. Vampire spawn. She will say anything to be let out.
- An empty crypt with fresh carving over the door. It carries the name of whichever character Strahd has liked best so far.

## Scene: The brother (K85)

> A white marble tomb behind a gate. On the slab lies a young man in
> shining armour, hands on his sword, unmarked by time. He looks as
> though he lay down an hour ago. He looks like the Count would, if the
> Count had ever been happy.

Sergei. The Tome told them who he was and what was done to him.

- The gate is heavy. Strength DC 20 to lift, or find the lever.
- His armour is the finest in the valley, plate +2. Taking it is not a sin. Sergei would have given it.
- Abraham's own suit was built for this work. Sergei's was built for a wedding, and it is better.
- If the Tome is here, it opens to its last written page. Below Strahd's hand, in a different one, is a single line that was not there before. Choose what Sergei says.
- Evander can feel that this one tomb is at peace. It is the only quiet place in the castle. A long rest here is safe.

## Scene: The parents (K88)

> Two great tombs side by side, a king and a queen in stone. The roof
> has come down across one of them. No one has cleared it.

King Barov and Queen Ravenovia. He has never repaired it.

- Torvald stands in front of a king who built a kingdom and a son who buried it. The stone king upstairs told him to count who stands with him. Here is the answer.
- Evander asked himself all night who made him. Here is who made the Count. The queen's face has been worn smooth by a hand touching it.
- The rubble hides something. This is a good place for a treasure your Tarokka reading has not yet delivered.

## Scene: Where he sleeps (K86)

> Down a last flight of steps, a tomb with no name on it. A black
> coffin stands open on a dais, lined with earth. Around the walls are
> three smaller alcoves, and their doors are ajar.

Strahd's tomb, and the brides who guard it.

- Three vampire spawn: AC 15, HP 82 each. For this party use two awake, and the third rising on round three.
- The stair down is trapped to throw intruders elsewhere. Run it from the book.
- The coffin is empty at night. If the Heart of Sorrow is broken and the sun is up, it is not.
- Spoiling the earth with holy water denies him his rest here. Evander knows the rite. It takes three uninterrupted rounds.
- Abraham knows the rest. Stake, sunlight, running water. This is what four years on the road were for.
- If Strahd is present he does not rage. He sits up, sees what they are holding, and says, "You read it. Then you know I have tried everything else."
- Gertruda, to the coffin: "Statement: I have a complaint about the workmanship." She will take the first shot if no one stops her.

## Secrets and clues

- Emil was jailed for refusing to hunt children. The pack leader who gave him up is still loyal to the Count.
- The second chair on the torture balcony was meant for Tatyana.
- A hunter of Abraham's blood got this far, lost, and was buried with honours. Strahd respects very few people. That was one.
- Patrina tried to marry the Count for his power and was stoned by her own people. Her brother still means to bring her back.
- Sergei's spirit is not in the tomb. It walks somewhere on the land, and it is waiting for the bride.
- The empty crypt has been recarved many times. There are older names under the new one.
- Strahd has not looked at his mother's tomb since the roof fell. He had it come down himself.
- The earth in the coffin is from the valley as it was before the mists. There is no more of it anywhere.

## Treasure and rewards

- Sergei's plate armour, +2.
- The hunter's silvered weapon and letter.
- Whatever the Tarokka reading placed in the catacombs.
- Emil's debt, or Zuleika's, if he walks out alive.
- A night's true rest in the one place in Ravenloft that allows it.
