# Session: The Spires of Ravenloft

Up from the Rooms of Weeping into the towers (K47 to K60, and the Heart of
Sorrow at K20). The red light in the window has been waiting all night.

The heartbeat is the engine. Everything up here pulses in time with it, and
whatever wounds the heart wounds the Count.

If the party still holds the Tome, Strahd is hunting and the towers are a
dead end he is happy to let them climb. If they gave it back, they are
guests again, and guests may wander.

Room numbers are from the book. It has the last word on traps and stats.

## Strong start: The portrait blinks

> The stair opens on a landing hung with one great painting. The Count,
> younger, in armour, one hand on a sword. The rug beneath it is deep red
> and very clean. As you step off the last stair, the painted eyes move
> to whoever carries his book.

K47. The portrait is a guardian and the rug is alive. They wake if anyone
touches either, or tries to pass with the Tome.

- Guardian portrait: AC 5, HP 22, cannot move, casts from the frame. Run its spells from the book.
- Rug of smothering: AC 12, HP 33. It takes whoever is standing nearest the wall.
- Abraham, Investigation DC 13: the rug has no wear. Nobody walks on it. Nobody who still walks, anyway.
- Gertruda: "Observation: the furniture is armed. Statement: I approve of this household."
- Cutting the canvas is loud. If Strahd is hunting, he arrives one scene sooner.

## Scene: The lounge (K49)

> Velvet chairs, a cold hearth, and a handsome man lying full length on a
> divan with a glass he is not drinking from. He does not get up. "Oh,
> thank the dark. Company."

Escher. Vampire spawn, consort, bored past bearing and afraid he is being
replaced. He would rather talk than fight.

- Vampire spawn: AC 15, HP 82. He fights only if cornered or insulted.
- He wants gossip. What did the Count say at dinner? Did he look at anyone for long?
- He trades truth for attention. One answer per good story, and Batrdlebee gets two.
- He knows the heart in the tower takes the Count's wounds for him. He will say so if he is made to feel pitied, and regret it at once.
- To Evander, delighted and cruel: "Half! How economical. Do you get half the hunger, or all of it and half the teeth?"
- He will not help against Strahd. He will, however, forget to mention he saw them.

## Scene: The broom and the guest room (K50, K51)

> A bedchamber made up for visitors, with turned-down sheets and a
> basin of water gone to ice. Your names are written on a card on the
> pillow. All of them. The ink is dry.

A place to rest, offered in bad faith. A short rest here is safe. A long
rest is interrupted, your choice by what.

- The closet next door holds a broom that attacks whoever opens the door. AC 15, HP 17. It is a joke. Let it be one.
- Torvald breaking a broom over his knee is a fine moment. Give it to him.
- The card lists Gertruda last, in a different hand, under the word returned.

## Scene: The witches' tower (K54, K55, K56)

> Cats. Dozens, on every stair, all of them watching you with the same
> flat patience. Above, women's voices and the smell of something boiling
> that used to be several things.

Barovian witches at their cauldron, with their familiars below. They serve
the Count for scraps of power and they are not brave.

- Barovian witch: AC 10, HP 16. Seven is the book number. Four is enough if the party is tired.
- The cats run to warn them. Stealth past the cats is DC 14, at disadvantage for anyone in metal.
- The witches bargain before they fight. They want hair, a tooth, a true name. They offer a potion, or the way to the heart.
- Evander's symbol frightens them more than Torvald's axe. They have seen axes.
- Batrdlebee can pass as a client. Witches love a customer.
- In the element room, among the jars: a brass gear, labelled with a girl's name and a date. Gertruda takes it without a word and does not say where it goes.

## Scene: The clockwork fool (K59)

> The top of the high tower is open to the weather through a broken
> roof. In the corner, sitting very still among the leaves, is a doll
> the size of a child, dressed as a jester. Its painted smile is
> cracked. Its head turns toward you with a small clean click.

Pidlwick II. A clockwork copy of a long-dead fool. It cannot speak. It
wants to be liked and it pushes people down stairs.

- Pidlwick II: AC 14, HP 10. Harmless in a fight, deadly at the top of a staircase.
- It attaches itself to whoever is kind to it, and waits for a high place.
- Gertruda meets something made the way she was. "Query: is this what I am to you? Clarification: answer carefully."
- If the party is kind to it in front of her, she softens by one degree. If they smash it, she notes how quickly.
- It knows the castle. It will lead them to the heart if asked with gestures, by the worst possible route.

## Scene: The Heart of Sorrow (K20)

> The stair winds up inside a hollow tower, and in the middle of the
> shaft hangs the thing you saw from the wall. A crystal heart, taller
> than a man, glowing red and beating. Each beat shakes dust from the
> steps under your feet.

The set piece. While the heart is whole, wounds dealt to Strahd go to it
instead.

- Heart of Sorrow: AC 15, HP 50. It hangs ten feet out from the stair. Reaching it means a thrown weapon, a shot, a spell, or a jump.
- The jump is Athletics DC 15 to land and cling. Failing is a long fall. Torvald will want to. Let him know the odds.
- Each round the heart is attacked the stair bucks. Dex save DC 10 or fall prone, and prone near the edge is a second save.
- The halberds on the wall are its guards. Use four, flying, AC 17, HP 17, +3 to hit for 1d10+1.
- Evander's pulse is its pulse. Every hit on the heart costs him 1 hit point, and he has advantage on attacks against it. Ask him if he keeps going.
- Gertruda finally gets to be loud. "Statement: at last."

When it breaks, the red light goes out all over the castle.

> Far below, something screams. It is not pain. It is the sound of a man
> who has just been made mortal by his dinner guests.

Strahd comes at once, with whatever spawn are near. This is the fight if
the party wants it, and the only time tonight he is truly angry.

## Scene: The bridge (K57, K58)

> A stone span between two towers, no rail, slick with rain. The
> courtyard is a long way down. Halfway across, the wind stops.

The way out of the spires, and the place Strahd catches them if they are
running.

- Crossing in a fight: Dex save DC 10 each round to keep footing, or drop prone.
- If the heart is broken he fights here himself, on foot, like the soldier in the portrait.
- If the heart is whole he does not bother. He stands at the far end and asks, pleasantly, where they thought they were going.
- Esmeralda's rope, if she is still in play, hangs from the far tower.

## Secrets and clues

- The heart was a gift from the powers he made his bargain with. It is not his heart. His own stopped at a wedding.
- Escher has seen the Count weep once, in front of the portrait in the study.
- The witches have been asked to brew something for a guest with half a vampire's blood. They do not know why.
- Pidlwick II pushed the first Pidlwick down the stairs. Nobody ever asked it why.
- The gear from the witches' jars is from Gertruda, taken out when she was made. She runs perfectly well without it. She would still like it back.
- The portrait on the landing was painted from life, before. It is the only image in the castle where he looks tired.
- Someone has climbed the high tower recently, by rope, from outside.

## Treasure and rewards

- The witches' shelf: two potions of healing and one unlabelled bottle. The unlabelled one is your choice.
- Escher's rings, if he is destroyed. He would much rather give one away for a compliment.
- A shard of the heart, if it is broken. It is warm, it still beats faintly, and Strahd will always know where it is.
