# Session 1: Arrival at Saltmarsh

The party arrives by the evening ferry. Rain. The harbour bell is silent,
which the locals notice and the party will not, yet.

## Strong start: The ferryman will not take their coin

> The ferryman holds up a hand before you can pay. "Keep it. You'll need it
> for the priest." He will not say more, and poles away into the dark.

If pressed: he lost a son to the chapel. He is afraid, not rude.

## Scene: The Drowned Anchor inn

Mira the fence is in the corner booth. She wants the party to notice her
without approaching. She knows where the bell went.

- Rooms are 5 sp. The innkeeper overcharges strangers by double.
- A notice on the wall: "Lost: church bell. Reward from Father Orm."
- If anyone asks about the chapel, the room goes quiet.

## Scene: Father Orm at the chapel steps

> The chapel door is chained from the outside. The chain is new.

Orm admits the bell was stolen a week ago and the tides have been wrong
since. He offers 50 gp and a blessing. He is lying about the blessing.
